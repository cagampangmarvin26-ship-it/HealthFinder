CHECK YOUR BENEFITS

May PhilHealth ka? You may qualify for covered care.

Registered members may access eligible services through a PhilHealth-accredited provider and the applicable primary-care program (YAKAP). Coverage is not an automatic discount on every hospital bill.

Explore YAKAP benefits ↗ Find accredited providers

Before your visit, ask the facility whether it is accredited for the service you need, whether you must register with a YAKAP clinic, and what documents or possible co-payments apply.

  • Confirm your registered primary-care provider.
